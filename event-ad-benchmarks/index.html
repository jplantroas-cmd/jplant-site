Event ad benchmarks

What Facebook and Instagram ads actually cost to sell event tickets. Every number below comes straight from the Meta ad accounts of campaigns we ran for ticketed events: concerts, festivals, club nights and college events.

The short answer

Across our 26 best published event campaigns, it cost a median of $3.36 in ads to sell one order, on a median order of $32.77. That works out to about 10.4% of the order going to ads. Across every campaign we have ever run, winners and losers together, the blended return is 6.79X.

How these numbers are measured

All figures are Meta-reported website purchases and purchase value from Ads Manager, using the default attribution setting, for the whole campaign. Return on ad spend is purchase value divided by ad spend. Cost per order is ad spend divided by purchases. These are our published case studies, so they lean toward wins. For the honest all-in number, use the blended 6.79X, which counts every campaign.
