Event ad budget calculator

How much should you spend on Facebook and Instagram ads to sell your event? Put in your goal. The math uses real results from ticketed events we have run.

How it works

Ticket revenue divided by return on ad spend gives the ad budget. The safe plan uses our all-time blended return of 6.79X, which counts every campaign we have run. The strong plan uses the median of our published event wins, 9.63X. See the raw data in the event ad benchmarks.

What moves the number

Tracking that counts every purchase, a real announcement-to-sellout plan, fresh ads, and money moved daily toward the ads that sell. Weak tracking alone can make a good campaign look like a loser.
