How we sell tickets

Tracking first. Every ticket purchase is counted back to the ad that sold it, on your ticketing platform. Without that, the ads are guessing.

A plan from announcement to sellout. Budget is set for the whole run of the event and scaled on a schedule, heaviest where tickets actually sell.

Several campaigns, each with a job. New audiences, people who already showed interest, and people who look like your buyers each get their own campaign, so we can see what is working.

Money follows the winners. We check results every day and move spend toward the ads and campaigns that are selling.

How much do Facebook ads cost to sell an event ticket?

In our published event campaigns the median ad cost was $3.36 per order on a $32.77 median order, about 10.4% of the order. Full numbers are on the event ad benchmarks page.

How much should I spend on ads for my event?

Start from your ticket goal and ticket price. Our free event ad budget calculator does the math using real results.

When should event ads start?

At the announcement. The first days of sales and the final two weeks sell the most, so the budget should be ready for both.

Do you work with ticketing platforms like Eventbrite, Dice, Posh or Tixr?

Yes. What matters is that every purchase is tracked back to the ads, so we set up tracking with your ticketing platform first.

Do you only work with events?

No. Events are where we proved it because they are the hardest thing to sell on a deadline. The same method works for any business that sells.
